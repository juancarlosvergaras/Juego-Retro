Generado con image_gen integrado. Referencia: soldado-v2.png. Archivo: soldado-agachado-pasos-v1.png.

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for the existing 2D pixel-art game Cap Dollar. Use the attached soldier sheet as strict identity/style reference: same stocky adult soldier, olive helmet, olive tactical uniform, small backpack, tan face and hands, brown boots, detailed pixel shading and dark outline. Create ONLY six sequential frames of a complete crouch-walking cycle to the RIGHT. Layout exactly 3 columns by 2 rows, evenly spaced equal cells, transparent RGBA background, no ground, no shadows, no labels. All six poses remain LOW in a deep crouch with knees bent and torso leaning slightly forward, hands close together at chest (empty hands, no gun). Obvious alternating steps: 1 front boot planted forward rear leg bent behind, 2 weight transfer rear boot lifts, 3 rear foot passes under hips, 4 opposite boot planted forward, 5 other rear boot lifts, 6 other foot passes under hips. Keep head, helmet, body proportions, costume and color identical between frames; steady low head height, feet on consistent baseline per row. Whole characters fully inside cells with generous transparent margins. Crisp chunky pixel art matching the reference, not smooth painted/cartoon art. Do not stand upright, jump, crawl on hands or kneel still. The foot and knee positions must visibly differ across every successive frame.
