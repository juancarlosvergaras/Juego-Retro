## Prompt del tripulante

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a NEW game sprite sheet matching the supplied image's exact detailed pixel-art enemy soldier style, colors, helmet, red scarf and dark gray military uniform. The reference is style/character reference only. Transparent RGBA background, no text, no grid, no scenery, no tank, no rifle, no shadows on ground. 1536x1024 image, exactly 8 equally-sized 384x512 cells, 4 columns by 2 rows. Same character side view facing RIGHT in every cell, waist-up tank commander, consistent head/body size across all frames and belt baseline at cell y=440; entire waist-up body visible in every cell, generous margins. In order: (1) idle, both hands on unseen hatch rim at waist; (2) looking right holding small olive grenade at chest; (3) pulling pin at chest; (4) windup, grenade hand behind head raised high; (5) throw release, throwing arm fully extended forward/right, empty hand, no airborne grenade; (6) follow through leaning forward; (7) retracting arm and gripping unseen hatch rim; (8) crouched shoulders and helmet descending toward waist baseline. Crisp pixel clusters and black outlines, textured metal helmet, cloth shading, readable anatomy, visually polished side scrolling arcade sprite animation. No floating props, no guns, no drawn hatch; all opaque pixels belong to the soldier or held grenade.

## Prompt de la explosión

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for a detailed pixel-art side-scrolling military arcade game. A polished ROCKET / GRENADE EXPLOSION, exactly 8 frames arranged in a 4-column 2-row grid on a genuine transparent RGBA background, 1536x1024 pixels, each cell 384x512. Every explosion centered at x=192,y=256 within its cell with clean transparent margins; same camera/scale. Read left to right top to bottom: tiny white-yellow impact flash with sparks; growing asymmetric orange-white fireball; maximum bright explosive fireball with jagged tongues and metal sparks; rolling orange-red fire and dark lobed smoke; fire fades into large dark brown/gray smoke; ember-filled smoke expands; smoke dissipates; sparse fading wisps. Detailed pixel clusters, hard pixel edges and textured shading, dramatic yellow hot core, burnt orange layers, gray charcoal smoke. Absolutely no floor, no stones, no ground rubble, no vehicle, no character, no object, no text or grid. Explosion must work in mid-air as well as on a wall. Keep every frame isolated entirely inside its cell. No solid background or fake transparency checkerboard.
