Generado con image_gen integrado. Archivo: crater-ciudad-v1.png. Agua, reflejos y salpicaduras animados por el juego.

Use case: stylized-concept. A production environment sprite for a detailed pixel-art side-scrolling military action game, palette matching ruined gray brown urban masonry and asphalt, realistic arcade pixel shading. One wide shallow bomb crater in a destroyed street, STRICT flat SIDE VIEW cross-section for a platform game, NOT a top-down aerial ellipse. Transparent RGBA background. Wide horizontal asset roughly 4:1. Broken dark asphalt road lips at left and right at the SAME height; jagged concrete chunks and rubble around both rims; the terrain slopes gently down from each edge into a long low flat muddy stony bowl, then up at the other edge. Crater is about three soldier lengths wide and knee-to-waist deep, shallow and safely walkable. Interior empty and dry with visible darker mud and small rubble; NO water because animated water will be composited by game engine, no sky, no city, no characters, no text, no shadow outside the asset. Leave a broad clear open horizontal cavity between the two rims for water overlay; no tall rocks in the center. All artwork entirely inside frame. Pixelated crisp detailed stone and cracked asphalt, not smooth painting.
